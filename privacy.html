kilo-joule privacy policy

Effective November 9, 2026

kilo-joule plans cycling and strength training, daily calorie and macro targets, meals and grocery lists. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer receives none of your data.

Phone-only mode (the default)

Everything you enter and everything the app computes (your profile, weigh-ins, training plan, meal plans, grocery lists, sales you mark) is stored only in the app's storage on your phone. The app makes no network requests in this mode. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes all of it.

What the app can read on your phone, and why

Each of these is optional and asked for only when you turn it on in Profile. You can turn it off there, or revoke the permission in Android settings, at any time.

DataUsed for
Calendars you choose (read only)Finding your work shifts and busy times so training and meals fit around them. Only the calendars you pick are read. The app never writes to your calendars.
Health Connect: weightYour weight trend, which sets your calorie targets.
Health Connect: exercise sessions, power, active and total calories burnedMarking planned workouts as done and fueling the day for the energy you actually spent.

This data is processed on your phone and is never sent anywhere, sold, shared, or used for advertising. kilo-joule's use of information received from Health Connect follows the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Server mode (optional)

You can instead connect the app to a kilo-joule server that you run yourself. In that mode the app sends your requests (viewing days, logging weight, checking off groceries, settings) only to the server address you entered. That server is under your control. The developer does not operate one for you and has no access to it. In server mode the phone does not send calendar or Health Connect data; the server reads its own sources as you configure them.

Children

kilo-joule is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Not medical advice

The targets are planning estimates for healthy adults, not medical advice. Talk to a clinician before changing how you eat or train if you have a medical condition.

kilo-joule

Training, fuel and meal prep planned around your shifts.

kilo-joule is an Android app that plans your week of cycling and strength training around when you actually work, whether that's 9 to 5 or rotating hospital shifts. It sets each day's calories and macros from the training, and turns them into meals, batch-cooking days and a grocery list.

  • Hard sessions land where there's time and recovery; long rides on days off.
  • Daily calorie and macro targets follow your training and weight trend.
  • Recipes portioned to each day's numbers, batch-cooked on the days you choose.
  • A body map to give muscles more work, or to work around a sore one.
  • Reads your work calendar and Health Connect, if you allow it.

No account, no ads, no tracking: everything stays on your phone.
